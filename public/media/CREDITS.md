# Demir Digital visual assets

All four project images are original, procedurally modeled 3D concept visualizations created for this website. Geometry, surface materials, staging, lighting and camera composition were authored for this project and rendered with Blender 4.3.2 / Cycles. No external stock photographs, client screenshots, trademarks, logo files or purchased assets are incorporated.

- `limon.webp` — Original travertine restaurant interior with architectural arch, olive branches and ceramic tableware. Hospitality design concept.
- `seyban.webp` — Original titanium turbine-wheel study with individually modeled spokes, machined rings and recessed fasteners. Automotive design concept; not a manufacturer product photograph.
- `ciger.webp` — Original terraced olive landscape with sculpted fields, planting rows and a winding limestone road. Agriculture / commerce design concept.
- `demir.webp` — Original continuous titanium ribbon with obsidian monolith and cyan light. Demir Digital studio design study; not an official brand logo.
- `showreel.webm` — Original 8-second, silent, 1280 × 720 / 24 fps editorial film built from these four concept images, animated crops and studio typography. It depicts design studies, not completed client work.
- `reel-poster.webp` — Still from the original showreel.

The reel typography uses the locally installed DejaVu Sans font (DejaVu Fonts / Bitstream Vera derived, permissively licensed). Font outlines are rasterized into the video and poster; no font files are redistributed. See https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html for the font license.

Recreate the reel with `python scripts/make-reel.py` from the repository root (Python, Pillow, ffmpeg and the locally installed DejaVu font family required). It uses only the local WebP images and makes no network requests.

The Limon, Seyban and Ciğer names label conceptual portfolio studies supplied in the creative direction. These assets must not be presented as photographs, official identities, verified engagements or evidence of business results.
